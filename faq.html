Help Topics & Quick Answers

Find clear answers regarding orders, international shipping, sizing specifications, and return processing.

Once your order has been dispatched from our fulfillment facility, an automated shipping confirmation email containing your direct parcel tracking link and carrier identifier will be sent to your registered email address. You can also view live delivery milestones directly using your unique tracking code on the carrier portal.

We support standard payment gateways including Visa, MasterCard, American Express, and Discover credit/debit cards. All transactions are processed using 256-bit SSL encryption to ensure end-to-end payment security.

Standard domestic orders typically arrive within 3 to 5 business days following dispatch. Express expedited delivery options guarantee arrival within 1 to 2 business days. Comprehensive shipping tiers and international rates are detailed in our dedicated Delivery Information guide.

Yes, we offer insured international logistics to numerous countries. International delivery takes approximately 6 to 14 business days depending on customs clearance procedures at destination ports.

Items may be returned within 30 days of delivery date provided they remain unwashed, unworn, and accompanied by original packaging and tags. For full terms, claim eligibility, and processing timelines, please review our Refund Policy.

Upon receiving and passing warehouse inspection, refunds are issued to the original payment method within 3 to 7 business days. Banks or card issuers may take an additional 2 to 5 business days to post the balance to your account statement.

Detailed measurement specifications (chest, waist, hip, and length in inches and centimeters) are provided on every product catalog page. If you are between two sizes, we generally suggest selecting the larger size for a relaxed drape or the smaller for a tailored fit.

Most of our premium cotton and blended garments are machine-washable on a gentle cold cycle with like colors. We advise air drying flat or tumble drying on low heat to maintain textile integrity and prevent shrinkage over time.
