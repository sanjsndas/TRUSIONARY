How to Measure Chest

Wrap a soft measuring tape horizontally around the fullest part of your chest, under your arms, keeping the tape parallel to the floor.

How to Measure Waist

Measure around your natural waistline, which is the narrowest point above your hips and below your ribcage. Keep one finger between tape and body.

How to Measure Inseam

Take a pair of your favorite well-fitting pants. Measure along the inner leg seam from the crotch point straight down to the bottom hem.
